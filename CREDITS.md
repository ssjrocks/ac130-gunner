# Credits

The game, its terrain, buildings, effects and sound are made in code. The 3D
models below come from Sketchfab under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were adapted for
the game by `tools/models/convert.py`: rescaled, reposed (arms lowered or put
in a rifle grip), decimated, and their textures rebaked into one texture each.
